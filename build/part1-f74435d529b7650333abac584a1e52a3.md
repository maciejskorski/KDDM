:::{note}
**Limitations of the KDD Model**

The model has three notable shortcomings {cite}`hofmann2003kdp`:

- **Incomplete lifecycle** — deployment and monitoring are absent; discovered patterns may never reach production or get validated in the real world.
- **No business perspective** — domain knowledge and stakeholder goals are not part of the process; a technically correct model may answer the wrong question entirely.
- **No agility** — the pipeline is linear with no inner iteration loops; adapting to new requirements means restarting from scratch.
:::
