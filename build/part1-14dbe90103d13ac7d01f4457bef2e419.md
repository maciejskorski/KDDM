From raw data to actionable knowledge: the KDD pipeline comprises selection, preprocessing, transformation, mining, and interpretation — each step potentially feeding back into earlier ones {cite}`fayyad1996kdd`.
:::

```{note}
**Critique of Fayyad et al.'s KDD Model**

While foundational, the model has notable limitations (Hofmann, 2003):
it lacks a business perspective and does not address completeness or integrity
requirements for successful projects; it is rigid and difficult to adapt to changing
requirements, with no inner iteration loop until the evaluation stage; and it ignores
the involvement of human resources throughout the process.
```
